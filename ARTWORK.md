# Artwork record

Three project illustrations were generated using the built-in imagegen tool (not the CLI) and copied into the public website. These are atmospheric interpretations of the campaign notes, not authoritative maps. Original outputs remain in the tool's generated-images folder.

## site/assets/evershadow-city.png

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape hero illustration for an elegant fantasy tabletop campaign city guide. Primary request: Evershadow, a vast inhabited high fantasy city built in five concentric rings around the Abyssal Core, a monumental dark-stone and enchanted-metal circular dungeon entrance opening down into a deep teal-lit abyss. View from a high rooftop overlooking the city at blue-hour dusk, intricate dense stone architecture, arcaded civic halls around the central plaza, guild halls and needle towers, distant layered outer districts fading into mist, fine threads of illuminated arcane transit, tiny warm amber windows. Painterly realism, finely detailed traditional fantasy book-cover artwork, atmospheric volumetric mist, restrained dark forest green and charcoal palette with aged gold light. Cinematic wide panoramic composition, the imposing circular central entrance is in the right half with varied towers and terraces around it; left half atmospheric rooftops and shadow with enough quiet area for text overlay. The city is on the surface under an open cloudy dusk sky; dungeon goes below it. Awe and the promise of adventure, inhabited and majestic rather than ruined. No typography, no letters, no watermark, no modern technology, no UI. Aspect ratio 16:9, high resolution.

## site/assets/verdant-meadow.png

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape banner artwork for a fantasy tabletop dungeon field guide. The Verdant Meadow, first floor of the Abyssal Core: rolling lush grassland, tall grass, scattered groves of old trees, a narrow stream and a well-trodden path winding from foreground toward hills. One distant tree with large natural bee hives; a small grove with faintly glowing leaves. An otherworldly landscape concealed inside a vast magical dungeon, its horizon hazy and ambiguous. Peaceful beauty with the suggestion of hidden danger. Fine painterly realism, traditional fantasy book illustration, muted olive green, warm golden sunlight, misty atmospheric depth, tactile detail, no characters in foreground. Wide cinematic landscape, no text, labels, UI, watermark, or architectural ruins. 16:9.

## site/assets/whispering-woods.png

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape banner artwork for a fantasy tabletop dungeon field guide. The Whispering Woods, second floor of the Abyssal Core: extremely tall ancient forest trees, a winding narrow path disappearing into dense mist, tangled roots, a small clearing with faintly glowing mushrooms. Fine painterly realism, traditional fantasy book illustration, desaturated deep forest greens and blue-gray mist, tiny pale warm glowing fungi, atmospheric shafts of dim light, detailed bark and ferns.
