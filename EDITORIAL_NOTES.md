# Campaign owner’s editorial notes

This document is outside `site/` and is not included in the player zip. Original campaign notes were left unchanged.

## Publication boundary

The website contains a deliberately curated public edition. It never reads the vault at runtime. NPC entries use visible appearance, public role, reputation, location, and practical reasons to approach them. They exclude statistics, hidden identities, private motivations, secret relationships, and quest outcomes.

The Saints appear only as the publicly known charitable organization. Their connection to the Syndicate, the Synners, the Veil’s network, and hidden vaults is excluded. The political entry does not reveal covert faction affiliations. If your players already know these relationships, they can be added deliberately later.

The public `Adventurer Guidebook/Floors 1 and 2.md` is the authority for floor content. Known creatures, drops, safe-route cautions, and locations from that guide are included; combat statistics, encounter rolls, unpublished floor maps, and later-floor material are excluded. The city-art illustration and floor-art illustrations are atmospheric interpretations, not navigable maps.

### Conflicting or incomplete draft details

1. **District dimensions:** Core notes order the districts as Central, Trade, Residential, Artisan/Academic, Outer. Other planning notes place Residential at 45–75 miles or 54–80 miles and assign incompatible widths. The atlas uses the consistent five-ring order and explicitly says it is schematic, not to scale. Exact ring distances are omitted. The 15 million population is a planning estimate.
2. **Empty district files:** The dedicated Trade/Artisan/Outer headline notes contain no text. Their city entries rely on `Core City details.md` and relevant guild notes rather than adding unsupported specific businesses.
3. **Commoner budgets:** Several narrative example totals do not equal their line items. The skilled worker example sums to 17 gp, not 15; the merchant/guard example to 35, not 30; the administrator example to 110, not 90. These examples are omitted. The explicit wage and rent tables are retained.
4. **Adventurer income:** One note suggests 500–1,000 gp/month for successful adventurers on floors 1–2, another suggests 50–100 gp for low-ranked adventurers. Neither range is presented as a promised or normal income.
5. **Loot settlement:** Explicit 50% sale value, 5% guild fee, and 10% city tax are used. The notes do not define whether taxes are assessed on the offer, appraisal, or after another deduction. The estimator clearly states its chosen convention: both fees on the merchant offer, rounded individually to copper. For one 150 gp Bear Hide: 75 gp offer − 3.75 gp fee − 7.50 gp tax = 63.75 gp net.
6. **Enchantment prices:** An early services list calls basic enchantment 200 gp; the detailed crafting breakdown specifies 200 gp materials + 300 gp labor = 500 gp for a basic +1 weapon enchantment. The website uses the detailed total and labels its components.
7. **Aetheric Marks:** No conversion rate is defined against gp. The site describes the system without inventing an exchange rate.
8. **Political office:** The politics note labels Darius Blackthorn as being from the Adventurer’s Guild; other sources consistently identify him as Merchant’s Guild leader. His player profile uses Merchant’s Guild leader; the generic Board description avoids assigning the inconsistent affiliation or chairmanship. The Central District overview also uses “Council of Elders”; the dedicated political structure note’s “Board of Directors” is used for the website.
9. **GrinReapers leader:** The explicit leader section identifies Retsu “Reaper” Jinsei, while the old concluding paragraph says Korrin Steelstrike. The explicit leader section is used.
10. **Ranks:** `Guild Promotions.md` describes review-based progression; `Badges Overview.md` adds floor-clearance milestones and deeper-zone details. The site uses the reviewed F–S progression system and does not turn floor clears into automatic promotions or expose later sections.
11. **Thorn / Thorne:** These are kept as separate characters: human Guildmaster Thorne Ironguard and dwarf advisor Thorn Ironfist. Likewise, Elara Windrider and Elara Stormveil remain separate public profiles.

Player-facing original connective text is written to summarize the notes. The two checklist entries for party coordination and badge/permit preparation are practical reminders derived from the guild and dungeon-entry notes, alongside the guidebook’s equipment recommendations. No new quest postings or claimable rewards have been invented.
